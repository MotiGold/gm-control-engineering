WHAT WE DO

One team, from copper to control loop

We cover the full stack of a real-time product: the board it runs on, the firmware that drives it, and the control software that keeps it stable under load.

Real-Time Systems Design

Architecture for systems that must respond within hard or soft deadlines — where a late answer is a wrong answer.

  • Requirements & timing budget analysis
  • RTOS selection & task scheduling
  • Control loop & sensor/actuator integration
  • Safety, redundancy & failure-mode design

Electronic Board Design

Schematic capture and PCB layout built for signal integrity, EMC, and manufacturability from prototype to production.

  • Schematic capture & component selection
  • Multilayer PCB layout & routing
  • Power, analog & mixed-signal design
  • Bring-up, test & DFM/DFT review

Firmware & Control Software

Low-level firmware and the control software layered on top — written for determinism, testability, and long-term maintainability.

  • Bare-metal & RTOS-based firmware
  • Device drivers & communication stacks
  • Closed-loop control algorithms
  • Diagnostics, logging & field updates

How a project moves

A short, direct path from concept to a validated system — with hardware and software developed in lockstep, not in sequence.
